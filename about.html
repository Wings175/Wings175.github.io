About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, working with Professor Zuankai Wang in the Nature-Inspired Engineering Laboratory.

I earned my Ph.D. in Wetting Dynamics of Non-Newtonian Fluids at Tsinghua University. During my doctoral studies, I spent a year as a visiting researcher in Professor Nenad Miljkovic’s group at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, investigating droplet dynamics. Following my Ph.D., I pursued postdoctoral research on vitrification, crystallization of liquid materials, and novel cryopreservation techniques for biological materials and plant embolism, collaborating with Professor Zuankai Wang (The Hong Kong Polytechnic University) and Professor Chenjie Xu and Professor Steven Wang (City University of Hong Kong).

My research spans (but is not limited to) three key areas:

  • Wetting of Complex Fluids
  • Freezing at Capillary Scales
  • Biophysics in Cryopreservation
